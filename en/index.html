Typisch Posener

Published October 2019 (in German)

Julius Posener's finest legacy may well be his language. Through his writing he saved buildings and changed the face of Berlin, without ever building himself. Katrin Voermanek follows the traces of the architectural historian, critic and activist, who died in 1996. Through nine stories about individual buildings, she traces how Posener went about his fight for the built heritage and how he voiced his criticism of new construction: at times impulsive, at times strategic, always grounded in a thorough education, with plenty of humour and a gentle verbal power that remains unmatched to this day. The short narratives, which deal with the Schaubühne, the Künstlerhaus Bethanien, the Kino Babylon and two villas by Hermann Muthesius, among others, bring the building scene of Berlin from the 1960s to the 1990s to life and demonstrate both the necessity and the effectiveness of committed architectural criticism.

Das Liebling Haus
Edited by Sharon Golan / Ellen Kugler / Shira Levy Benyemini
Editing and interviews: Florian Heilmeyer and Katrin Voermanek

The Liebling Haus in Tel Aviv was built in 1936 to designs by the architect Dov Karmi for Tony and Max Liebling, a couple who had fled Europe. With its light plastered façades, deep loggias, clear proportions and a floor plan oriented to the city's climate, it is a prime example of the International Style adapted to local conditions that is typical of Tel Aviv's White City. From 2015 to 2019 the house was painstakingly restored by Israeli and German planners, conservation experts and craftspeople and reopened as a lively cultural and exhibition centre. The book, published in three language editions, tells the story and development of the house and its residents through texts, interviews and the atmospheric photographs of Yael Schmidt.

Consulting | PR

Katrin Voermanek advises and writes for architecture firms, companies and institutions: since 2003 as a freelance associate for Barkow Leibinger, and also, among others, for Leon Wohlhage, LIN, Regina Poly, Kirsten Schemel and Ludes Architekten, the architecture gallery "framework", Eternit, Schindler and USM. She has given seminars for the IFBau of the Architektenkammer Stuttgart, led architecture tours for, among others, the Baden-Württemberg state representation, the Hapag-Lloyd Cruises club and the Architektenkammer Hessen, and was a member of the curatorial board of commissioner-general Francesca Ferguson for the contribution "Deutschlandschaft" to the 2004 Architecture Biennale in Venice.
